Trump, Xi and Putin hold their first three-way summit

The US, Chinese and Russian leaders met for 90 minutes at Kylin Villa, Shenzhen, on the 33rd APEC summit’s sidelines. Their four-point “Declaration toward Peace”: UN reform, more refined petroleum, ICC restraint and a dialogue on reducing nuclear risks.

2   THE HAGUE · JUSTICE

World Court finds Myanmar guilty of Rohingya genocide

The ICJ ruled 9–6 that Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya in Rakhine State in 2017, and ordered it to investigate, prosecute and pay compensation. No amount was set. Myanmar must file a report within two years.

— AP, BBC, HRW
